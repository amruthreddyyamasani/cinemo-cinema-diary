# Cinemo UX design brief

Cinemo is for people who want more from a movie tracker than a list of watched titles. They want to remember what a film did to them, find the next film with a reason, and test whether their cinema knowledge is real or mostly confidence.

The product goal is to make the first useful action feel immediate while giving the brand a memorable entrance. The opening cinematic is a brief invitation into a screening room; the catalogue and diary that follow are calm, searchable, and practical. The hero earns its space through the product metaphor, not through decorative spectacle.

The experience principles are: content before chrome; recommendations must explain their evidence; personal data must be real and local to the current user; motion must reveal structure rather than delay it; and every animated surface needs a static, keyboard-accessible equivalent.

The key emotional progression is curiosity, entry, recognition, action, and return. A visitor scrolls into the screen, sees that the screen is actually a door into Cinemo, searches or browses a real film, logs it, and gets a better next choice over time.
