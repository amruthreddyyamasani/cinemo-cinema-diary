# Cinemo screen specs

## Cinematic entry

Purpose: establish the product metaphor and move visitors into Cinemo. Primary action: Enter Cinemo. Secondary action: native scroll. Desktop behavior: pinned 170vh section with a scale push toward the centered screen and a bridge reveal. Mobile behavior: static poster, readable headline, and direct entry without a fragile 3D camera move.

## Home and discovery

Purpose: continue from the entry into useful cinema exploration. Primary actions: search, browse the reel, open film details, and enter the knowledge quiz. The conventional search input and catalogue remain the accessible alternative to the signature reel.

## Film details

Purpose: make a real film actionable. Metadata includes poster, year, synopsis, runtime, genres, director, cast, language, country, source, and personal actions. Missing imagery and unavailable data are not fabricated.

## Library

Purpose: manage watched, watchlist, and favourites shelves. The empty state tells the user exactly how to fill the current shelf. Rating controls use half-star behavior and remain keyboard labelled.

## Knowledge

Purpose: assess demonstrated recall. The result shows correct answers out of five, percentage score, explanation, and methodology note. CountUp is only used for the real percentage.

## Settings

Purpose: control theme, account mode, and local data. Destructive clearing is explicit and local. The page does not imply server sync where no provider exists.

## Current screen additions

**Library:** three derived stats, shelf tabs, title filter, sort selector, rating controls, and private review editing from the film modal.

**Home:** recommendation actions are View film, Not for me, and Go wider when a genuinely unfamiliar genre is available.

**Quiz result:** percentage, correct-answer count, restart, and privacy-safe share action. The result explicitly states that knowledge is based on balanced recall rather than watch count.
